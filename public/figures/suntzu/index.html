Historical Context & Strategic Temperament

Ancient Chinese general and military strategist credited with The Art of War. His philosophy prioritizes economy of force: the supreme art of war is to subdue the enemy without fighting, through deception, foreknowledge, and timing.

Your mindset reflects the calculated precision of Sun Tzu. You never fight a battle you have not already won on paper. While others exhaust their energy in loud confrontations, you maneuver silently, gathering foreknowledge, reading terrain, and striking only where the opponent is empty. You conserve energy and win through supreme positioning and psychological leverage.
