ST

Sun Tzu

The Master Tactician

Ancient Chinese general and military strategist credited with The Art of War. His philosophy prioritizes economy of force: the supreme art of war is to subdue the enemy without fighting, through deception, foreknowledge, and timing.
MA

Marcus Aurelius

The Stoic Emperor

Roman emperor who governed at the height of imperial power while enduring constant pestilence, warfare, and betrayal. His personal journal (Meditations) codifies radical acceptance of external events and unwavering mastery over internal thoughts.
MC

Marie Curie

The Relentless Innovator

Pioneering physicist and chemist, the first person to win two Nobel Prizes in different scientific fields (Physics and Chemistry). Curie persevered through poverty, institutional sexism, and physical hazard to isolate radium and inaugurate the atomic age.
LD

Leonardo da Vinci

The Polymath

Italian Renaissance genius whose unquenchable curiosity refused disciplinary boundaries. He fused optics, hydrodynamics, anatomy, and engineering with peerless painting, illustrating that innovation occurs at the intersection of diverse systems.
